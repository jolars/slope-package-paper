\begin{letter}{Computational Statistics \& Data Analysis}
  \opening{Dear editor,}

  Please consider our manuscript, ``Efficient Solvers for SLOPE in R, Python,
  Julia, and C++,'' for publication in \emph{Computational Statistics \& Data
  Analysis}.

  The paper presents a suite of packages for fitting generalized linear models
  with the sorted $\ell_1$ penalty. A shared C++ library supplies the solver to
  R, Python, and Julia. The implementation supports dense, sparse, and
  out-of-memory data, full regularization paths, cross-validation, and relaxed
  SLOPE. It extends the previously published hybrid coordinate descent method
  with screening rules and other implementation improvements.

  We evaluate the software in reproducible Benchopt experiments on simulated
  and real data. The benchmarks compare both individual solutions and full
  regularization paths at a common duality-gap threshold. We also illustrate
  the packages in a metabolomics analysis. We believe the combination of
  statistical software, numerical algorithms, and empirical evaluation fits
  the journal's Computational Statistics section.

  The manuscript and its reproducibility materials are available at
  \url{https://github.com/jolars/slope-package-paper}. The software packages
  are available through their respective package repositories.

  The manuscript has not been published elsewhere and is not under
  consideration by another journal.

  Thank you for considering our submission.

  \closing{Yours sincerely,\\[1ex] Johan Larsson, Ma\l{}gorzata Bogdan, Krystyna
    Grzesiak, Mathurin Massias, and Jonas Wallin
  }

  \encl{manuscript and replication materials}
\end{letter}
